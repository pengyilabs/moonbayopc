Why Suzhou?

Suzhou Industrial Park (SIP) is one of China's most successful economic zones, consistently ranked as the top destination for foreign investment. Located just 30 minutes from Shanghai, SIP combines world-class infrastructure with an exceptional quality of life.

  • 30 minutes from Shanghai by high-speed rail
  • Over 180 Fortune 500 companies have operations here
  • Special tax incentives for OPC-registered businesses
  • World-class healthcare, education, and international schools
  • 2,500-year cultural heritage alongside cutting-edge innovation
