2026 SuKeSong Hackathon — 48 Hours to Build in Suzhou

On October 30 – November 1, 2026, 200+ creators gather on the shore of Dushu Lake for 48 hours of building across three AI tracks, competing for a ¥500,000 prize pool. Recruitment is open now.

Date
October 30 – November 1, 2026
Format
48-hour hackathon
Location
Dushu Lake, Suzhou Industrial Park
Organisers
36氪 · 锦丰集团 · 浙江大学校友会

SuKeSong (苏客松) is a large-scale creator hackathon held on the shore of Dushu Lake in Suzhou Industrial Park. Around 200 creators — university students, independent makers, AI developers and product managers — team up for 48 hours of non-stop building. The total prize pool is ¥500,000.

Please note: this is an external event. Moon Bay OPC is sharing it with our community but is not the organiser. Registration, the participant QR codes and the group chat are all handled by the organisers — use the official announcement linked below.

The three tracks

  • AI + Smart Manufacturing — industrial quality inspection, predictive maintenance, supply chain and agents
  • AI + Healthcare — medical imaging, drug discovery and health management
  • AI + Work & Life — smart offices and meetings, voice interaction, home, consumer, education and city services

Schedule

  • Oct 30 — opening ceremony, company challenge statements released, Founder open mic, team formation
  • Oct 31 — immersive build day with workshops and on-site mentors
  • Nov 1 — submissions close at 11:00, followed by the Demo Market and closing ceremony

About 80% of the challenges come from real business scenarios set by companies and research institutes in the district. Alongside the three tracks the programme includes one Founder open mic, three workshops, a two-day future-scenario pop-up exhibition and a Demo Market.

What participants get

  • Free meals and accommodation for the duration of the event
  • Compute and hardware support — LLM APIs, dev boards and sensors, toolchains
  • Direct access to real industry problems, plus investor matchmaking
  • Media exposure, project showcase and venture connections from 36氪

Who it is for

University students, independent creators, AI developers and product managers. As the organisers put it: if you are curious about technology and serious about making things, you can sign up. Sponsorship and brand partnership slots are also open.

Registration is open

Sign-up, the registration QR codes and the participants' group chat are all in the official announcement on WeChat.
