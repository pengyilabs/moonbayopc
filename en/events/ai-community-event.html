The AI Wave Is Here — Suzhou AI Practitioners Share Their Real-World Journey

Four practicing AI builders from Suzhou talk honestly about going from idea to a live online product — what worked, what didn't, and how small teams are shipping with AI in 2026.

Date
September 19, 2026
Time
13:30 – 16:00
Location
Dongyan Siji Apartments, Suzhou
Cost
Free · Register on WeChat

This is our first offline community event, co-hosted by Moon Bay OPC and the Hengtai Zero-Space OPC Community. The idea is simple: instead of more theory, we invited people who have actually shipped things with AI to walk us through their real journey.

Who should come

  • Founders and solo operators exploring AI-driven products and one-person companies
  • Foreign-trade and manufacturing businesses curious about practical AI applications
  • Professionals in Suzhou who want to meet other builders and practitioners
  • Anyone seeing the AI wave and wondering where to start for real

Speakers

ZY
Zhang Yulei
"The 82 Principle — a new thinking paradigm for the AI era"
Partner at Singapore's Valtimus and Suzhou's Minnas, with a Harvard and McKinsey background. Shares how to decide where to put your effort — rather than trying to do everything yourself.
WR
Wu Runyu (悟云)
"One person + AI: how far can you actually go?"
Since January, he has made AI the backbone of his operation — workflows, content operations, vibe coding, and product development. Explores how tiny teams can do what used to take many people.
Z
Ji — Founder, Zhitu AI (智图 AI)
"Industrial AI landing: the Zhitu 3D smart review system"
A hands-on industrial AI product built on a domestic engine with secure, private local deployment — a real-world example made for the manufacturing sector.
XJ
Xu Jinyang
"An AI cross-border client-acquisition system"
Built specifically for foreign-trade companies to find overseas customers and close deals, with at least one completed transaction already under its belt.

Plus 1–2 more speakers to be announced. There's no marketing talk — just real practitioners showing what they built and how.

Join us — it's free
