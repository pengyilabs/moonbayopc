OPC Community Signs Cooperation Agreement with SIP Talent Apartments

The official signing ceremony between the Moon Bay Overseas Talent OPC Entrepreneurship Community and Suzhou Industrial Park talent apartments — formalizing housing support for international entrepreneurs.

Date
July 26, 2026
Type
Signing Ceremony
Location
Suzhou Industrial Park
Partners
Moon Bay OPC · SIP Talent Apartments

Full coverage of the official signing ceremony between the Moon Bay Overseas Talent OPC Entrepreneurship Community and SIP talent apartments. The ceremony established a formal partnership to provide housing for overseas entrepreneurs, creating a seamless integration of entrepreneurial support and living accommodations.

What happened

  • The Moon Bay OPC community and SIP talent apartments signed a formal cooperation agreement
  • Establishment of a dedicated housing pathway for international entrepreneurs in Suzhou
  • A gathering attended by representatives from Suzhou Industrial Park management, talent apartment operators, and OPC community members

Why it matters

The partnership is expected to serve as a model for other innovation parks across China looking to attract and retain international entrepreneurial talent.

This signing ceremony represents a key milestone in Moon Bay OPC's mission to create a comprehensive support system for international entrepreneurs, covering everything from company registration to daily life needs.
